ABOUT THE TOPIC

What is Travel & Tourism?

Travel means moving from one place to another, while tourism is the activity of visiting places for leisure, business, culture, education or other purposes. Tourism creates jobs, supports local businesses and helps people understand different cultures.

KNOW MORE

Types of Tourism

Tourism can take many forms depending on the purpose of travel.

🏛️

Cultural Tourism

Exploring history, traditions, festivals, art and heritage.

🏔️

Adventure Tourism

Activities such as trekking, rafting, camping and mountain sports.

🌱

Eco Tourism

Responsible travel that protects nature and benefits local communities.

🏥

Medical Tourism

Travelling to another place for healthcare and medical services.

🏖️

Leisure Tourism

Travelling for relaxation, holidays, beaches and entertainment.

💼

Business Tourism

Travel for meetings, conferences, exhibitions and professional events.

TRAVEL SMART

Simple Travel Guide

01Plan your trip — Choose your destination, budget, dates and transport.
02Pack wisely — Carry documents, medicines, suitable clothes and essentials.
03Respect local culture — Follow local rules and respect people, traditions and places.
04Travel responsibly — Avoid littering and protect natural and historical sites.
05Stay safe — Keep important documents secure and stay aware of your surroundings.
